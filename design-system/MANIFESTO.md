# Gradus

Gradus is an overall fitness app for the average user. It aims to provide one place where the user
can see their health metrics, plan training for various sports, and track their progress.

On the technical side, Gradus is an end-to-end encrypted vault for health-oriented data, with open
standards for fitness and health data. The user can be sure that, with us, their data is not used for
tracking, selling ads, or analytics, and they can also self-host Gradus if they wish. All our code is licensed under AGPL-3.

Gradus should look and feel like a calm, objective, precise, professional, and trustworthy training app
with no gamification. There's no compromise when using Gradus. The user pays a monthly fee or self-hosts the server
to have their data backed up and receives no ads, social gamification, or external pressure on their training.
It is ideal for people who are not into social media.

## Persona

Meet Brenno, Gradus's example persona. Brenno is a programmer who, a few years ago, started to care about his health
and started to do some exercise. His doctor recommended that he do two types of exercise:
- Gym / weight lifting
- Some cardio activity, like running, CrossFit, or something similar

So, he enrolled in a gym and found that he had no support for understanding his progress. So, he needed either to hire a
personal trainer or to use an app like Hevy to track his workouts. Similarly, for cardio activities, he needed something
like Runna or had to pay for professional help to progress in running.

Being a programmer, Brenno also cares about his privacy. He avoids using too much social media and has privacy concerns
about those platforms. He's also not happy that his health data lives in one app, gym exercises in another, and running
activities in a third one. He would prefer to have everything centralized in one app.

Brenno is what we'll call an "average user": someone who is working toward being healthy and progressing in his
exercises, likes to beat his personal bests and share his progress with family and friends, but is not looking to
become an athlete and has other responsibilities in life.

Gradus aims to be the perfect app for this average user by consolidating all health and activity data in one vault,
so the user can plan their workouts end to end in one place.
In Gradus, the user will be able to:
- See their health metrics, like sleep, heart rate, and others, and identify patterns
- Plan and track their gym workouts and make progress
- Plan and track their outdoor activities and make progress

Gradus is not meant for professional or semi-professional athletes; it's unlikely that we'll provide the deep level
of insight required for this vertical, given our privacy constraint.

## Greek culture

We draw our design inspiration from ancient Mediterranean (Greek/Roman) culture. We are inspired by the ancient Olympic Games,
where people gathered in various competitions to see who was the best in each category. We believe that practicing
sports is more of a battle against oneself than against others, so we believe that every person on their journey is living a
"personal Olympic game" where the goal is to beat their past self.

Gradus means "step" in Latin, reflecting this principle. Step is a metaphor for progress and also the base measure that every
fitness tracker collects from a person. We aim to be the go-to app when a person wants to see their health metrics and
make progress in their exercises. Step represents that.

For the app aesthetic, we prefer colors like beige, terracotta, and variations of brown. We draw inspiration from Greek marble statues and temples,
ancient pottery, desert landscapes, and the Colosseum.

## Design Principles

- Gradus is focused on self-comparison. We deliberately avoid social features and aim to compare a person with their past self
- Gradus is for amateurs. We aim to digest health data into easy-to-understand charts and information. We create indexes and
aggregations so people know what is going on, with an optional deep dive for those who want the full picture
- Gradus is for people who want to celebrate. We track a person's personal bests in their exercises and provide ways for that person
to share the goals they reach with external platforms and friends in a privacy-respecting way
- Gradus is for people who care about their privacy. Gradus is private by default; a user does not need to enroll in advanced
protection. It's the opposite: a user opts into features that weaken their privacy, like sharing data, not the other way around
- Gradus uses objective language: usually a motivational phrase or summary grounded in reality, plus data that shows that reality
