  1. 01
    Keep two reps in reserve.Effort

    Use the proposed load as a guide. Reduce it if you could not complete two more clean repetitions after the target.

  2. 02
    Brace before the bar moves.Technique

    Before each squat and Romanian deadlift set, take your position, breathe in, and create steady trunk pressure before beginning the repetition.

  3. 03
    Let readiness set the rest.Recovery

    The rest interval is a reference, not a deadline. Begin the next set when your breathing is controlled and you can repeat the previous set’s technique.

  4. 04
    Why the large lifts come first.Curiosity

    Squats, presses, and hinges ask for more coordination. Placing them before accessory work helps preserve the quality of the main training.
